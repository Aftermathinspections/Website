Read the full service agreement
  1. Services. AIS performs the plan review and/or required building code inspections for each permit Contractor assigns to AIS, under s. 553.791, F.S.
  2. Contractor's duties. Contractor files the Notice to Building Official with the owner's acknowledgment, keeps its license and insurance current, gives AIS safe access to the work, and requests inspections through the AIS app or by email.
  3. Scheduling. AIS schedules requests in the order received, within its daily capacity, and may reschedule for weather or safety.
  4. Results. AIS issues a written result for each inspection. Work that does not pass is not approved until corrected and reinspected. Same-day reinspections are at no charge.
  5. Fees and payment. Fees are as quoted in writing per job. AIS invoices about halfway through the job and at final.
  6. Insurance. AIS carries the insurance required by s. 553.791, F.S. Contractor keeps general liability and workers' compensation (or an exemption) in force and lists AIS as certificate holder.
  7. Scope. AIS inspects visible, accessible work for code compliance. AIS does not supervise the work, act as engineer of record, or guarantee the work.
  8. Term. This agreement covers every job Contractor assigns to AIS until either party ends it in writing. Jobs already started are completed under these terms.
